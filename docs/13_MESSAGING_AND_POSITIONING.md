## Brand Identity

**Product name:** IntelliStamp  
**Company:** IntelliCal Labs  
**Tagline:** *Digital loyalty stamps for every local business*  
**One-line pitch:** Replace your paper punch card with a smart QR-based loyalty system — no app required for your customers.

## Core Value Proposition

IntelliStamp turns every customer visit into a data point. Local businesses — cafés, salons, gyms, restaurants — gain the tools that enterprise loyalty platforms offer, without the complexity or cost.

**For business owners:**
- Know who your regulars are
- Reward them automatically
- Bring back customers who haven't visited in a while

**For customers:**
- No app to install
- No card to carry
- Works on any smartphone browser

> "Works on any phone. No app. No download. Customers scan, earn, and redeem — in seconds."

### Pillar 2: Know Your Customers

> "Every scan creates a customer record — phone, visit count, last visit. Turn anonymous foot traffic into known regulars."

> "QR codes rotate every 30 seconds. Screenshots and shared codes are worthless."

> "Works on Indian mobile numbers, designed for Indian cafés, salons, and gyms. WhatsApp-first campaigns, not email newsletters."

Use when: Local market positioning; differentiating from US/EU competitors.

### Pillar 5: Live in 10 Minutes

> "Set up your loyalty programme before your next customer walks in. No IT. No contract. No hardware."

Use when: Acquisition campaigns; countering objections about complexity.

## Audience-Specific Messaging

### Café Owner

**Pain:** "30% of my customers forget their punch card."  
**Hook:** "Your customers keep their phone. They never forget it."  
**Proof:** Every stamp is stored digitally — if they lose their phone, they re-enter their number and their stamps are still there.

---

### Salon Owner

**Pain:** "I don't know how to track this and I don't have time to learn."  
**Hook:** "Hand it to your receptionist. They learn it in 2 minutes."  
**Proof:** Kiosk mode — staff enter phone number, stamp is issued. No training required.

---

### Gym Owner

**Pain:** "I want to reward members who hit their 50th visit but I have no way to track it."  
**Hook:** "Set a milestone: 50 visits = free month. IntelliStamp tracks it automatically."  
**Proof:** Milestone rewards trigger automatically at visit thresholds. No manual tracking.

---

### Restaurant Owner

**Pain:** "My slow Tuesday lunches are killing me."  
**Hook:** "Send a WhatsApp blast to customers who haven't visited in 30 days — with one click."  
**Proof:** Campaign audience filter: "inactive customers (30+ days)" — message sent with your offer.

### Objection Handling

**"My customers aren't tech-savvy enough."**
> Actually, that's our most common concern — and it's never been the real problem. Scanning a QR with the camera is something every smartphone user already does. If they can use WhatsApp, they can use IntelliStamp.

---

**"I already use [competitor]."**
> That's great — what do you like about it? [Listen] The main difference with IntelliStamp is [relevant gap]. A lot of businesses switch to us because [specific advantage relevant to their complaint].

---

**"It's too expensive."**
> The free plan is genuinely free — no credit card, no trial period. You get the QR stamping, the dashboard, the kiosk mode, and the Google Review bonus. Most businesses run on the free plan for months before they ever think about upgrading.

---

**"My customers won't scan a QR code."**
> Think about it this way — your customers scan QR codes for menus, UPI payments, and WhatsApp already. A loyalty stamp is just one more thing they already know how to do. We've seen adoption rates of 60–80% at busy cafés within the first week.

---

**"What if I already have paper cards outstanding?"**
> You can give those customers a head start — when they register by phone, you can let them enter their current count. The staff can issue bonus stamps in the dashboard. Or just start fresh — new card, new programme.

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | Get to the point. No jargon. | "Your customers scan, you get data. That's it." |
| Warm | We're a team that built this for businesses we love. | "The kind of loyalty your regulars deserve." |
| Confident | We know this works. | "Not a demo. A working loyalty programme. Live today." |
| Local | Built for India, by Indians. | "Works on every Indian mobile. WhatsApp-ready." |

### Avoid

- Corporate language: "enterprise-grade", "synergy", "leverage"
- Overpromising: Don't imply WhatsApp delivery is live until it is
- Negativity: Don't say competitors are bad — just say what we do better
