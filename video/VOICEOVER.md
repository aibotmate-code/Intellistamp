# IntelliStamp Demo — Voiceover Script

**Duration:** ~100 words · ~40–45 seconds spoken

---

> Every time a customer walks out the door, you want them to come back.
> IntelliStamp makes that happen — effortlessly.
>
> Merchants get a smart loyalty dashboard with a dynamic QR code.
> Display it at the counter, or go full kiosk mode on any screen.
>
> Customers just scan, enter their phone number, and collect a stamp.
> No app download. No friction.
>
> Each visit builds their digital loyalty card — with milestone rewards along the way.
> Even a Google review earns a bonus stamp.
>
> From your dashboard you can see every customer, every visit, every reward — in real time.
>
> IntelliStamp. Turn visits into loyalty.
> stamp.intellicallabs.com

---

**Tone notes:**
- Clear, warm, confident — not salesy
- Pace: medium. Pause briefly after "No friction." and after "in real time."
- Emphasis on: "No app download", "just scan", "milestone rewards", "bonus stamp"
- Final CTA spoken slowly and clearly

**Suggested voice profile:**
- Male or female, neutral accent
- Warm but professional — think premium SaaS, not hyperactive ad
